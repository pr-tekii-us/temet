Temet comes from the Latin temet nosce, "know thyself", the Roman rendering of the maxim inscribed at the Temple of Apollo at Delphi. The phrase also has a place in AI fiction: it hangs over the Oracle's kitchen door in The Matrix, where a machine urges a human toward self-knowledge. That double meaning suits this project, which begins as a hands-on journey into how language models and machine learning actually work and ends as an interactive art installation that makes those ideas visible. Studying how a machine learns is also a way of looking at how we think, and the installation will invite its visitors to do the same.
